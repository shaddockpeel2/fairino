  No colour-mapped region was altered, so this error can only come from the
  newly added zone validation. This proves the launch file emits the zone

NOT VERIFIED - runtime action round trip:
  In this environment DDS completes discovery but does not deliver data.
  `ros2 topic echo /fr3c_first_grasp/state` received nothing while the node
  published every 500 ms, and a goal to the unchanged pick_hold action also
  stopped at "Sending goal:" with no goal response. The same failure therefore
  affects pre-existing paths, so it is an environment limitation rather than a
  regression. Runtime goal handling of pick_place (INVALID_ZONE,
  ZONE_UNAVAILABLE, slot selection, actual placement) still has to be
  exercised on the real setup. See the field checklist in
  docs/voice/P2_颜色抓取任务Action接口.md.

Known limits carried forward:
  - Zone slot occupancy only knows obstacles present in the MoveIt scene.
    Cubes the software never observed are invisible to it.
  - pick_place does not check that a colour matches a zone by design; a red
    cube may be placed in zone D.
  - The gripper remains open-loop; a successful pick_place still means the
    motion chain completed, not that the cube was sensor-verified as held.
